❔ Help & guide

what this program can do, and how ← Home

THE VIEWER is your offline workshop assistant for the technical manuals. Press Ctrl+K anywhere to jump to anything. Below is everything it does — grouped by what you're trying to get done.

New here? The 30-second tour
  1. Search a part name, NSN, or symptom on the home page — suggestions appear as you type.
  2. Open a result to read the page; press Ctrl+F to find a term across the whole manual.
  3. Stuck on a job? Go to Solve it — it walks symptom → parts → how-to → tools → schematic.
  4. Print a Job packet to take to the bay, or open the Part dossier to see everything about one part.
  5. Got new manuals? Add documents scans, indexes, and OCRs them — no command line.
Find information
🔎Search
Part #, NSN, material #, or a key term — with instant offline suggestions as you type.
🔍Look-Alike Parts
Tell apart parts that look identical but aren't — by NSN, UOC (which vehicle), CAGEC, SMR.
📋Part dossier
Everything about one part on one page: references, figures, variants, procedure, schematic, 3-D.
🔤OCR / searchability
Find-in-manual (Ctrl+F in the viewer) searches scanned pages as OCR completes.
Do the job
🛠Solve it
From a symptom to a fix: likely parts → procedure → tools & cautions → look-alike check → schematic.
🔧How to do it
Step-by-step remove/install, the tools required, and the WARNINGs — pulled from the manual, cited.
🎞Visual steps
The same procedure as a big follow-along flow with cautions called out — easy at the vehicle.
🖨Job packet
A printable take-to-the-bay sheet: steps, tools, cautions, and the right NSN to order.
Graphics & diagrams
🔌Schematics viewer
Explore wiring/schematic sheets from any angle — tilt, mirror, clean, blueprint, loupe.
🧊3-D Library
Representative 3-D shapes of parts, lit and rotatable — a feel for the part beyond the photo.
⚡Circuit Lab
Build or trace a circuit on a real schematic and watch it run — a learning & display tool. modern browser
📊Ops dashboard
Engine health: mode, coverage per vehicle, recent runs, page cache, and a file-integrity audit.
Manage the corpus
➕Add documents
Point it at a folder of PDFs — it previews what's new, snapshots, then indexes AND OCRs them in one pass. No command line.
🩺Health & audit
See searchable coverage climb and catch any source files that have gone missing on disk.
Power tips
🔍Jump to anything
Tap the "🔍 Jump to anything" pill in the bottom-right corner of any screen — jump to any feature, part, or vehicle instantly. On a keyboard, Ctrl+K (Cmd+K on Mac) opens it too.
⚙Performance (RPS)
Runs full-speed on modern PCs and stays usable back to Win 7 / Vista. Settings → Performance to choose a mode.
Everything here reads your manuals read-only and links to the real cited page — the manual is always the source of truth. THE VIEWER is a faster way in, not a replacement. Confirm torque values, sequences, and every CAUTION/WARNING on the actual sheet before working.
