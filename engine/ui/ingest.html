How it works: a safeguard snapshot is taken, then the tested crawl pipeline walks the folder, adds new documents, extracts their text, and queues scanned pages for OCR. Nothing is deleted or overwritten (R1/R6). After indexing, run the GPU OCR to make scanned pages searchable.
